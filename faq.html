More Than Just Acting

Theater builds skills that last a lifetime. Here’s what your child will gain through our programs.

Confidence Building

We help shy children find their voice and outgoing children channel their energy into powerful performances.

Teamwork & Empathy

Putting on a show requires everyone to work together. Students learn to support each other and understand diverse perspectives.

Creative Expression

From improv games to scriptwriting, we provide a safe space where imagination has absolutely no limits.

General Questions

We offer specialized workshops for children and teens aged 4 through 17. Our classes are divided into specific age groups (4-6, 7-10, 11-13, and 14-17) to ensure age-appropriate learning and socialization.

Not at all! We welcome students of all experience levels. Our beginner classes focus on the fundamentals in a no-pressure environment, while our advanced masterclasses offer challenges for experienced young actors.

Students should wear comfortable, non-restrictive clothing that allows them to move freely (like sweatpants, leggings, and t-shirts). Closed-toe soft shoes or sneakers are mandatory for safety on stage.

Most of our standard weekend workshops last for 2 hours per session. Our summer camps run half-day (4 hours) or full-day (7 hours). Rehearsals for mainstage productions may require longer commitments.

To minimize distractions and allow children to fully express themselves without feeling self-conscious, our classes are closed to parents. However, we do host an "Open Studio Day" at the end of every term where parents are invited!

We maintain a strict ratio of 12:1 for older children and 8:1 for ages 4-6. Every class is led by a lead instructor and supported by an assistant teaching artist, ensuring every child gets individualized attention.

Enrollment & Policies

Registration can be completed entirely online via our Booking portal. You will need to create a Parent Portal account, select the desired class, and submit the online registration form along with the initial deposit.

You may cancel up to 14 days before the start of a term for a full refund (minus a $25 processing fee). Cancellations within 14 days are eligible for studio credit only. Once classes begin, no refunds are issued.

No hidden fees! Tuition covers all class materials. For end-of-term performances, costumes are provided by our wardrobe department. We may occasionally ask parents to provide basic items like black leggings or plain t-shirts.

Yes! We offer a 10% discount on tuition for a second child, and 15% for a third. We also run an Early Bird registration period one month before the new term begins, which automatically applies a 5% discount at checkout.

If your child is sick or unable to attend, please notify us via the Parent Portal. We offer two "make-up" sessions per term by allowing students to drop in on an equivalent class on a different day, subject to space availability.

Absolutely. All of our 12-week courses culminate in a final showcase for family and friends. Additionally, our "Production Class" casts students in full-length plays and musicals that run for a weekend in our main theater.

Before You Ask...

Here are some quick answers to our most common queries.

What are your hours?

Our administrative offices are open from 9 AM to 6 PM on weekdays. Workshops typically run in the afternoons and on Saturday mornings.

How do I book a trial class?

You can easily book a trial class by creating an account on our Parent Portal, navigating to the Schedule tab, and selecting "Book Trial".

Do you offer parking?

Yes, we have a dedicated drop-off zone and a secure underground parking facility free for all parents for up to 2 hours during class times.

Is financial aid available?

Absolutely. We believe every child deserves the stage. Please email our support team for our scholarship and sliding-scale fee application.
